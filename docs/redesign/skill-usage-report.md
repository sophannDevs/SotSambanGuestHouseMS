## 3. Conflicts encountered and how they were resolved

1. **`ui-ux-pro-max` design-system output vs. the existing codebase's tokens.** Resolved in favor of the more specific, already-in-place evidence (the app's own `globals.css`/`tailwind.config.ts`) over the tool's generic pattern-matched suggestion — full reasoning in `design-system.md`.
2. **`frontend-design`'s "take a risk / signature element" stance vs. `impeccable`'s Operate-mode restraint and the brief's own "not ERP-complicated, calm, professional" requirement.** Resolved in favor of `impeccable`'s Operate framing and the brief, since both are more specific to *this* surface type (internal daily-use software) than `frontend-design`'s general marketing-site guidance.
3. **`impeccable`'s own bootstrap (`PRODUCT.md`/`DESIGN.md` via `init`) vs. the user's explicit `docs/redesign/*.md` deliverable list.** Resolved in favor of the user's explicit, more specific instructions (see table row above).
